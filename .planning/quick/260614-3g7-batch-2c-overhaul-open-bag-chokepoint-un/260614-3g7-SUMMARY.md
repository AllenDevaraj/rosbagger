now there is ONE `rosbagger_core.reader.open_bag()`. Consequences: **`bagq edit` and the TUI

lacked — edit raised, the TUI crashed), custom-type bags still teach (CLI-04), and no open
path leaks sub-readers (newE19). Full offline suite **655 passed, 6 skipped**.
